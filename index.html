Our Story

Born from a Passion for the Perfect Cup

Brewhaus was founded in 2012 by two friends who believed that great coffee was worth traveling the world for. We visited remote farms in Ethiopia's Yirgacheffe valleys, Colombia's cloud-forested mountains, and Yemen's ancient terraces — forming direct partnerships built on respect and fair pay.

Every bag that leaves our roastery carries the farmer's name on the label. Because behind every great cup is a human story — and we think that story deserves to be told.
